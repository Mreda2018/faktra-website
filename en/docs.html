One full cycle

Everything in Faktra turns around one cycle. Understand it and you understand the system; every other module hangs off these points.

  1. A customer and an item. The customer with their TRN, and the item with its price and tax category.
  2. A quotation. Optional, and it becomes an invoice in one click without retyping.
  3. A draft invoice. Edit it freely. It has no legal number yet and is not in the books.
  4. Issuing. Everything happens at once: a sequential legal number, the journal entry, the stock movement, cost of sales, and the e-invoicing XML. One transaction — all of it, or none of it.
  5. Collection. A payment or a cheque. The invoice closes and the entry is posted.
  6. Reports. The VAT return, ageing, profit, stock — all built from the same data, not from separate tables.
One rule that matters: Once issued, an invoice is never edited. Corrections go through a credit or debit note — not to be awkward, but because that is what makes your books survive an audit.

Invoicing

A tax invoice that meets UAEEgyptian requirements, Arabic and English on the same page, with a QR code. Numbering is sequential and cannot gap or repeat.

Types
Invoice, simplified invoice, credit note, debit note, quotation, recurring invoice, advance invoice
Discounts
Per line or per document, by percentage or amount
Currencies
Any currency; the ledger records the book currency at the invoice-date rate
Example — an invoice with a discount
Olive oil 500ml × 40 at 37.001,480.00
Line discount 10% −148.00
Net before VAT 1,332.00
VAT 5% 66.60
VAT 14% 186.48
Total due 1,398.60
Total due 1,518.48

VAT is computed once per group, not per line. The difference is a fils or two — and that is exactly what makes the authority accept or reject a filing.

E-invoicing

Faktra produces a PINT AE XML file for every invoice, validates it before sending, and keeps an archived copy. An outbox tracks the status of each one and retries what fails.

Important to be clear about: Faktra is not an accredited service provider. Official reporting goes through a provider you contract with, and the system connects to it. Any software claiming to report officially on its own should be asked to show its accreditation.

Faktra builds the authority's document for every invoice and serialises it exactly as the specification requires — which is where all the precision sits: the authority verifies the seal against that string character by character, so turning 14.00 into 14 makes the document invalid even though the number is the same. It is then sealed, submitted, and followed to acceptance, and stays archived.

Important to be clear about: Egypt has no accredited service provider between the taxpayer and the authority: submission runs under credentials the authority issues in your name. The sealing certificate lives on a token in your office, and no server — ours or anyone's — can touch it, so a small agent on that machine receives the string and signs it.

The e-receipt

Selling to consumers is a separate track, not a flag on an invoice. Receipts are collected into batches, and one seal covers a whole batch rather than each receipt — so a till doing a hundred an hour does not sign a hundred times.

What is refused before issue, not after

An issued invoice already carries a legal number and a ledger entry; withdrawing it after the authority rejects it is a credit note and a conversation. So the refusal comes first: an item with no code, a company with no activity code, a party with no registration number, a credit note with no original invoice — and the message names the line at fault.

What is validated first

  • Both parties' TRN and its format
  • Line totals reconciling to the invoice total, to the fils
  • Tax groups agreeing with the rates declared
  • The original invoice existing when a credit note is issued

Designated Zones

This is the rule people get wrong most often in the UAE, because intuition says one thing and the law says another. Faktra knows the four rules and reviews every invoice against them.

The four rules
  • Selling into a Designated Zone is not an export. A company in JAFZA is a UAE company; a mainland sale to it is standard rated.
  • The rule covers goods only. Services supplied inside a Designated Zone are taxed like anywhere else.
  • Goods moving between two Designated Zones can fall outside VAT, under conditions.
  • Goods consumed inside the zone remain taxable even when the buyer is in a free zone.

You flag a customer or warehouse as being in a Designated Zone once, and the system reviews every invoice and tells you "this one is marked zero-rated while the rules say standard" — before it reaches the authority, not after.

The profit margin scheme

For second-hand goods — cars, equipment, furniture. The tax is on the difference between what you sold it for and what you paid, not on the whole price.

Example — a used car
Sold to the customer for 60,000.00
Bought for 56,000.00
Margin4,000.00
VAT inside the margin (5/105)190.48
What the customer pays60,000.00

A system that doesn't know the rule would charge 3,000 in tax — 2,809 too much on every car. And the printed invoice does not state the VAT amount, because stating it would let the buyer reclaim tax that was never charged.

And if a car sells at a loss the margin floors at zero — not a negative that nets off against another car that made a profit. That netting is tidy arithmetic and a misdeclaration.

Excise tax

For tobacco, energy and carbonated and sweetened drinks, and electronic smoking devices. The tax is a percentage of the published retail price — not your cost and not your selling price.

Example — importing an energy drink
Quantity imported 1,000
Published retail price per can12.00
Rate (energy drinks) 100%
Excise due 12,000.00
Less 100 cans re-exported−1,200.00
Net payable 10,800.00

If your selling price is 10, a system reaching for the price you already typed would compute 10,000 instead of 12,000 — two thousand short on one return.

Collections & post-dated cheques

Post-dated cheques are central in both markets and most systems get them wrong: either treating them as cash received, or ignoring them until they clear.

A customer hands over a 10,000 cheque maturing in 90 days
On receipt — the invoice settled
On receipt — the bank unchanged
On receipt — cheques receivable10,000.00
After 90 days when it clears — the bank10,000.00

So nobody chases a customer who has paid, and nobody spends money that has not arrived. If the cheque bounces the debt returns to the customer automatically and reappears in collections — with the bank charge alongside it.

There is a maturity calendar showing incoming and outgoing cheques by week, and a dunning process that sends escalating reminders by email or WhatsApp.

Bank & foreign currency

You import the bank statement and the system matches each line to a recorded payment. What doesn't match stays visible, and the reconciliation states its difference rather than hiding it.

Stock

Moving average cost, multiple warehouses, batches and expiry, and append-only stock movements — every movement is its own row and nothing is edited retroactively.

How the average moves
Receive 100 at 20.00 avg 20.00
Receive 100 at 24.00 avg 22.00
Sell 50 cost 1,100.00
Balance 150 units3,300.00

The cost of a sale is frozen on the movement when it happens. A later purchase moves the average forward but never rewrites a sale already made — if it did, every historic profit report would change behind your back.

Goods returns

Not everything that comes back goes back on the shelf. This module separates the goods from the money: the return owns the stock, the credit note owns the account.

A return of 11 units with three dispositions
6 good — back into stock + stock
3 seals broken — quarantined not sellable
2 crushed — scrapped written off

A crushed unit going back on the shelf is precisely the error this exists to prevent. And the scrapped cost is already in cost of sales from the original sale — booking it in and out again would double the loss.

Manufacturing

A work order draws materials, carries labour hours and overhead, and receives finished goods at their real cost. The variance between planned and actual is the reason the module exists.

A run of 20 chairs that yielded 18
Materials drawn 4,000.00
Labour 18h × 45 810.00
Overhead350.00
Total run cost 5,160.00
Cost per good chair (÷ 18)286.67

Divided by 18, not by 20. Dividing by the planned quantity would give 258 — stock valued too low and an overstated margin on every chair sold afterwards, forever, with nothing to flag it.

Leads & sales commission

A simple pipeline: a lead, a stage, an activity, then won or lost with a recorded reason. The reason matters — "price" on 40% of lost deals is a finding; forty different sentences saying so is not.

Commission is earned on collection, not on issue
The rep's invoice 50,000.00
Collected so far 30,000.00
Commission rate 2.5% —
Earned 750.00

Not 1,250. A business does not pay commission on money it has not collected — a half-paid invoice earns half.

Document expiry

The trade licence, VAT certificate, staff visas, labour cards, vehicle registrations, insurance. Each document with its own notice period.

A visa needs 90 days' notice; a vehicle registration 30 is enough. The screen respects that difference, and separates "already expired" from "due soon" — the first is a fine already accruing, the second is an appointment to book.

Health checks

This is what sets Faktra apart, and it is not a feature so much as a way of building. The dangerous accounting errors are not the ones that crash the program — they are the ones that pass quietly and surface a year later.

Four numbers whose only acceptable value is zero
Tenant isolation 0
Stock value vs the Inventory account0.00
Customer and supplier balances vs their control accounts 0.00
Unbalanced journal entries0

Anything other than zero names a path that changed one figure without telling the other side. These run against your own data and you can look at them any time — not a promise in a brochure.

Security & backup

  • Database-level isolation: each company's data is separated by rules inside the database, not a filter in the code — so even a programming mistake cannot let one company see another's data.
  • An audit log with a verifiable chain: every change is recorded and linked to the one before it, so deleting a row breaks the chain visibly.
  • Encrypted backups: daily and automatic, with a second copy off the server. Restores are rehearsed on a test database — a backup nobody has restored is not a backup.
  • Roles and permissions: who sees what and who approves what, with multi-step approvals for large transactions.

Migrating from an old system

The most dangerous moment in an accounting system's life is its first day. The migration screen imports customers, suppliers, items, the chart of accounts, opening balances and historic invoices.

The part that matters: Duplicate invoices are detected before they land. An invoice recorded in the old system and again on import would be counted twice in the VAT return and twice in revenue — the kind of thing found at audit a year later.

Opening balances post as a balanced opening entry — the trial balance balances from day one, and any difference shows immediately instead of being dragged along for a year.
