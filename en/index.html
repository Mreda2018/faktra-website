The problem

Most invoicing systems don't know UAE rules

These are not hypothetical. Each one costs money, and each one shows up at audit rather than when it happens.

VAT on the whole price of a used car

A dealer sells a car for 60,000 that cost 56,000. The tax is on the 4,000 margin — 190 dirhams, not 3,000. A system that doesn't know the margin scheme overcharges the buyer by 2,810 on every car.

A free-zone sale treated as an export

Selling into a Designated Zone is not an export, and the rule treats goods and services differently. Faktra knows the four rules, reviews every invoice against them, and tells you when one disagrees.

A post-dated cheque counted as cash

A cheque settles the invoice the day it is handed over, but the money only reaches the bank ninety days later. Faktra keeps the two apart, so nobody chases a customer who has paid and nobody spends money that has not arrived.
