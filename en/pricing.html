Questions people ask

Is Faktra an accredited service provider?

No. Faktra generates the PINT AE invoice, validates it, archives it and tracks its status. Official reporting goes through an accredited provider that you appoint, and the system connects to it. Anyone telling you their software reports officially on its own, without an accredited provider, should show you their contract.

How does the document reach the authority in Egypt?

Egypt has no accredited provider between the taxpayer and the authority: the taxpayer submits directly, with credentials the authority issues to them. Faktra carries out that submission on their behalf under those credentials and follows the status through to acceptance or rejection. The electronic seal is the one part no server can hold — its certificate lives on a token in your office, so a small agent on that machine does the signing.

What do I need before I can submit to the authority?

Credentials the authority issues in your name, a nine-digit registration number with your activity code, and an electronic seal on a token. The screen names what is still missing one item at a time rather than saying "incomplete".

Is the e-receipt included at these prices?

Yes, on all three plans. It is a separate track from the invoice rather than a flag on one: receipts are batched and a single seal covers the batch, so the till does not stop at every sale.

What happens after the free trial?

The 14 days run without a card. If you continue, you pick a plan and your data stays exactly as it is. If you leave, you take a full copy of your data in a format any other system can read.

I have an old system — how does the data move?

There is a dedicated migration screen for customers, suppliers, items, the chart of accounts, opening balances and historic invoices. It detects duplicate invoices before they land, so nothing is counted twice in the VAT return or in the books.

Where is my data and who can see it?

Each company's data is isolated at the database level, not by a filter in the code — so even a programming mistake cannot let one company reach another's data. A check verifies this across every table and every view.

Can I change plan later?

Yes, at any time. Upgrading opens new modules immediately without losing anything. Downgrading closes the modules but keeps their data — come back to them later and it is all still there.

Do these prices include VAT?

Prices shown are before VAT. Your invoice will show the tax on its own line, as the law requires.
